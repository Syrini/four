Our story

GreenLeaf opened in 2019 with one goal: make it easy to eat and drink well without driving across town. Every supplier on our shelf is within a half-day's drive, and every recipe is one of ours.

We're open seven days a week, and every table is wheelchair accessible, as is our single-level entrance and restroom.
